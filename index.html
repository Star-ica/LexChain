How it works

01
📝
Draft your agreement
Choose a template or build a custom agreement from scratch. Add parties, clauses, jurisdiction, and terms.
02
🔒
Hash the content
The full agreement text is hashed with keccak256, producing a unique fingerprint that is stored on-chain as proof of content.
03
✍️
Both parties sign
Party A signs first, then Party B countersigns. Each ECDSA signature is verified against the content hash on-chain.
04
⛓️
Active on-chain
Once both parties have signed, the agreement status becomes Active and is permanently recorded on Botchain.

Built for trust

🔐
Content integrity
keccak256 hashing ensures the agreement text can never be altered after signing. Any change produces a different hash.
✅
ECDSA verification
Every signature is verified on-chain using ecrecover, proving exactly which wallet signed what content.
📋
Status lifecycle
Agreements move through Draft → Pending → Active states. Either party can void before both have signed.
🏛️
IPFS document storage
Full agreement documents are stored on IPFS / Arweave. The URI is recorded on-chain alongside the content hash.
⚡
Botchain native
Deployed on Botchain (chain ID 677) — fast finality and low fees for legal-grade transactions across Africa.
🔍
Fully auditable
Every action — creation, signing, voiding — emits on-chain events. The full audit trail is always available to both parties.
